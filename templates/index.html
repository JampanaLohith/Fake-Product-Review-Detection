How the Machine Learning Pipeline Works

Every submitted product review is processed through a multi-stage feature extraction pipeline before classification.

Extracts unigram and bigram word frequencies after NLTK tokenization and WordNet lemmatization, pinpointing deceptive marketing vocabulary and bot phrases.

Stylometric Statistics

Calculates structural metrics including punctuation density, capitalization ratios, review character length, and average word length to detect unnatural bot formatting.
